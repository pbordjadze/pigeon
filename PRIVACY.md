# Pigeon privacy policy

Last updated October 23, 2026.

Pigeon has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Its developer receives nothing from the
app: not your location, your routes, your drives or anything about how you use it.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your location.** Pigeon reads it to guide you and to notice when you leave a route. It is
  used on the device and is not stored after a drive except as described next.
- **Your routes and drives.** Each saved route (its path, start and destination, its name, its
  stage) and the record of your drives along it (whether each was clean, when you drove it) are
  stored in the app on your iPhone. Deleting a route deletes them; deleting the app deletes all of
  them. They are included in your iPhone's own backups like any app's data.

## What Apple Maps sees

Pigeon gets its routes, place names and map from Apple Maps (Apple's MapKit, built into iOS):

- When you look for a destination, what you type is sent to Apple as you type it, with the area
  the map shows, to suggest places.
- When you save a route, the route's start and destination are sent to Apple to work out the
  route and the landmarks along it, and a position may be sent to look up its street or place
  name.
- If you leave the route during a drive, your position and destination are sent to Apple to work
  out a new route.
- While the map is on screen, Apple's map tiles for the area you are viewing are loaded.

A drive along a saved route otherwise asks Apple Maps for nothing. Apple handles these requests
under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and says it does not associate
them with your Apple Account.

## Permissions

- **Location, While Using the App.** Needed to guide you. During a drive Pigeon keeps receiving
  your location with the screen locked (iOS shows that it is in use); it stops when the drive ends.
  Pigeon never asks for Always.
- Pigeon asks for nothing else.

## Children

Pigeon is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
